# 2010–2018 Ram HD A-pillar gauge pods

Research for a custom 3D-printed A-pillar pod on a 2017 Ram 3500 (4th gen, 6.7 Cummins; same cab as 2010–2018 Ram 2500/3500). The truck has a factory A-pillar grab handle. The custom pod is meant to hold a 1.28 in round LCD (about a 33–38 mm module), an EC11 rotary encoder, and a 0.36 in 3-digit LED voltmeter — smaller than the 52 mm (2-1/16 in) gauges these products are built for.

Prices and page contents were checked on Sunday, November 15, 2026 (America/New_York). Pages that returned HTTP 200 are marked as loaded. GlowShift product URLs were read successfully earlier in this session (full product text and schema price captured) but later returned HTTP 403 to a bare curl, which is a bot block, not a 404. No 60 mm A-pillar pod for this truck was found. No Dash Designs or Lumen A-pillar pod for 2010–2018 Ram was found. No Ram-specific A-pillar gauge pod was found on Printables or Thingiverse.

Nothing below invents a dimension or a mounting step that was not on a product page, official manual, or a photo saved in this folder.

## Common mounting approaches

Three commercial patterns showed up. Only the second is a close match for a clip-on or grab-handle-bolt custom part that keeps the factory handle.

1. **Full A-pillar replacement.** The stock pillar trim and the grab handle come off. A molded full-length cover with two or three 52 mm cups bolts to the body through a pre-drilled hole at the factory grab-handle mounting point, usually with a supplied bolt and a 4 mm Allen key. GlowShift, MaxTow, Auto Meter 17218/17219, and ISSPRO R77029 (from its product photo) are this style. These do not keep the factory grab handle.

2. **Add-on cup that bolts to the lower grab-handle hole, factory trim stays on.** Banks iDash Stealth Pods (63384/63385) are the clearest example: the pod bolts to the lower A-pillar grab-handle bolt hole, the factory pillar stays, and Banks says the grab handle is retained. A short supplied bolt is tightened to 8–10 in-lb. Wiring needs a hole drilled in the factory pillar. OZ Gauge Pods and carzdeluxe sell 3D-printed 52 mm cups with the same kind of mounting ear and a boss that indexes into the pillar, plus a supplied socket-head bolt. Their install photos show the stock pillar left in place. Those photos do not show a grab handle, and neither listing clearly says the handle is kept, so handle retention on the OZ/carzdeluxe design is unknown.

3. **Overlay the stock pillar and fasten through it.** Auto Meter’s generic “Full Pillar” sheet (2650-1643, still linked from the 17218/17219 pages) says the cover installs *over* the stock A-pillar, with four 3/16 in holes drilled through both pieces and ratchet fasteners. That conflicts with Auto Meter’s own “replacement pillar” product title and with retailer copy that says you swap the factory trim for this one. A 2010–2018-specific Auto Meter sheet that settles this was not found. Sunstate’s listing says “replace the factory pillar cover,” “no drilling into the factory pillar,” and includes “retention clips, hardware,” while also saying the pod does not block the grab handle. Sunstate’s product photo is a full-length dual trim with a mid-pillar bolt recess and no handle, so whether a factory handle can actually be reused is not shown.

No product in this set was documented as tape-only or as a pure friction clip with no bolt.

**Best starting point for a custom part:** the Banks / OZ / carzdeluxe add-on. It uses the existing grab-handle bolt, leaves the factory pillar (and, on Banks, the handle) in place, and is already a compact cup hanging toward the driver — a better scale for a 33–38 mm LCD, a knob, and a small voltmeter than a full replacement trim built around two or three 52 mm gauges.

## Airbag notes

These are manufacturer warnings, not a determination of what is on the owner exact truck.

- Auto Meter 2650-1643: “A-pillar gauge mounts should never be installed over an A-pillar or ‘curtain-side’ airbags.”
- GlowShift and MaxTow 2010–2018 pillar-pod instructions, step 1: “Prior to installing this pillar pod, ensure your vehicle does not have side curtain airbags.” Their pods are complete replacements of the factory A-pillar trim.
- Banks 4th-gen Stealth Pod install guide (part 63384/63385): keep the factory pillar; “you’ll be working near an airbag and you don’t want it to go off,” so disconnect both batteries before starting. Banks does not say the pod is approved as an airbag-safe modification; it only tells you the work is near one.
- ISSPRO, OZ, carzdeluxe, and Sunstate product pages checked for this research did not include a curtain-airbag warning. That is an absence of text, not a claim of compatibility.
- 2017 Ram 2500/3500 owner information describes side-curtain airbags (SABICs) that deploy downward over the side windows. Whether a given truck has them was not verified here. A design that leaves the factory pillar in place, as Banks does, is the approach the commercial products use when they acknowledge an airbag in that area. A full-replacement pod that the maker says not to install over curtain airbags is a poor starting point if the truck has them.

Product photo is a full-length trim with a mid-pillar bolt recess and no grab handle. - [Auto Meter 17219 full triple A-pillar, tan](https://www.autometer.com/2-1-16-trpl-replacement-pillar-dodge-ram-10-14.html), $104.54 (was $116.16), three 2-1/16 in (52 mm) holes, same years, same material and the same unresolved overlay-vs-replacement wording as 17218. Official page loaded (HTTP 200).

- [GlowShift GS-374G taupe triple pillar pod](https://www.glowshiftdirect.com/2010-2018-dodge-ram-cummins-compatible-2-1-16-taupe-triple-pillar-gauge-pod/), $66.99 (was $79.99), three 2-1/16 in (52 mm) holes. ABS plastic, taupe, factory color matched. Explicitly a complete replacement of the stock A-pillar and grab handle. Mounts with the included bolt into the grab-handle hole (4 mm Allen; GlowShift’s 2010–2018 guide says use the upper factory mounting location and do not overtighten or the pod will bow). Side-curtain airbag warning in the instructions. The dual version, [GS-274G](https://www.glowshiftdirect.com/2010-2018-dodge-ram-cummins-compatible-2-1-16-taupe-full-size-dual-pillar-gauge-pod/), was $64.99 (was $79.99), UV-coated taupe ABS, same replacement method; its photo clearly shows the mid-pillar mounting hole. Sister brand [MaxTow MT-374G](https://www.maxtow.com/taupe-triple-pillar-gauge-pod-for-2010-2018-dodge-ram-cummins/) is the same triple pod at $66.99, with the same install PDF wording (page loaded, HTTP 200). GlowShift and MaxTow share a West Berlin, NJ address.

- [Banks iDash Stealth Pod, 63385 dual / 63384 single](https://www.bankspower.com/products/63384-63385-idash-stealth-pods-09-ram), $129.00 dual and $109.00 single (official Shopify prices), 52 mm cups made for the shallow Banks iDash. Official page loaded (HTTP 200). Fits 2009–2018 Ram 1500, 2019–2023 1500 Classic, and 2010–2018 (install guide also says 2013–2018) Ram 2500/3500. OE-grade Polyamide-12, ships natural black, paintable. Bolts to the lower grab-handle hole, retains the factory grab handle, factory pillar stays on. A cable hole is drilled in the factory pillar (16 mm step for one iDash cable, 18 mm if a PedalMonster is used). Tighten the supplied pod bolt to 8–10 in-lb; over-tightening can break the plastic. Not guaranteed for mechanical or deep-set gauges. Banks install guide tells you to disconnect the batteries because the work is near an airbag.

- [OZ Gauge Pods 2009–18 Ram 1500/2500/3500 pillar gauge pod](https://www.ozgaugepods.com/product-page/2013-18-dodge-ram-1500-2500-3500-pillar-gauge-pod), $59.99, in stock. Page loaded (HTTP 200). ABS, black, paintable, for 2-1/16 in (52 mm) gauges. Listing text: “This is made go into your factory pillar,” “perfect fit, no modification required.” Photos show a 3D-printed dual cup and a single cup with a mounting ear, an index boss, and a supplied socket-head bolt, plus an install shot on a stock 4th-gen Ram pillar. Grab-handle retention is not stated, and the install photo does not show a handle. No airbag text on the page. OZ also lists a separate “Taupe Pillar Gauge Pod for 2010–2018 Dodge Ram Cummins” at $106.99, which was not opened in full for this note.

- [carzdeluxe dual grab-handle-cap bolt-on pod](https://carzdeluxe.com/products/custom-a-pillar-dual-gauge-pod-fits-2010-2018-ram-bolt-on), $55.00, sold out. Page loaded (HTTP 200). 3D-printed ABS, heat resistance listed as 212°F (100°C). Dual 52 mm cups (inside diameter 53 mm, outside diameter 61 mm, depth 55 mm), angled toward the driver, wiring access port, gauge bracket and foam strips included. Install tools listed: 10 mm socket with extension and a 4 mm Allen wrench. Photos show the same mounting-ear-and-boss idea as OZ, plus the back of the print and an in-truck side view on a stock 4th-gen pillar. Grab-handle retention is not stated; the install photos do not show a handle. No airbag text. Marked “for off-road use only.”

- [Sunstate Diesel SSD-APM-1318 A-pillar gauge mount](https://www.sunstatediesel.com/shop/a-pillar-gauge-mount-2013-2018-ram), $189.00, in stock. Page loaded (HTTP 200). 2013–2018 Ram 2500/3500 only (2010–2012 not listed). Two 52 mm (2-1/16 in) openings. OEM-matched molded ABS, factory texture and color. Listing says: replace the factory pillar cover, no drilling into the factory pillar, includes pod, retention clips, and hardware, and “holds two gauges without blocking the vent or the grab handle.” The product photo is a full-length dual trim with a mid-pillar bolt recess and no grab handle. Whether the factory handle can be reused is not shown. No airbag text on the page.

- [ISSPRO R77029 triple A-pillar, gray](https://www.isspro.com/aftermarket/r77029.html), $129.70, “TRIPLE (3) A-PILLAR, GRAY, FOR DODGE 2500-3500 2010-2018.” Page loaded (HTTP 200).
