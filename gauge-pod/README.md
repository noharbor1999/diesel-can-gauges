# A-pillar gauge pod

A-pillar 3-gauge pod for 75 mm round displays — coming soon.

The CAD for a 2010–2018 Ram HD A-pillar pod that holds the three Waveshare 2.1 in (75 mm-class) faces lives with another designer and will be added to this folder later.

Until then, see [BUILD_GUIDE.md](../BUILD_GUIDE.md) for mounting and airbag cautions, and `gauges/research/pods/PODS.md` for a research summary of commercial pillar-pod styles (Banks, Auto Meter, GlowShift, MaxTow, and others). Third-party instruction PDFs and product photos are not included here; use the manufacturer pages.
